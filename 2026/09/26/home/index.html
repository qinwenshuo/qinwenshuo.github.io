Wenshuo Qin

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Washington’s Paul G. Allen School, where I am advised by Prof. Max Kleiman-Weiner. My research broadly focuses on computational models of social cognition, with an emphasis on identifying the inductive biases that enable AI systems to align with human social judgments. In my prior work, I showed that relational and 3D representations play a critical role in enabling models to recognize social interactions in ways that more closely resemble human perception.

Previously, I was a research coordinator at Johns Hopkins University, where I worked with Prof. Leyla Isik. I earned B.S. degrees in Neuroscience and Computer Science from Boston College, where I conducted research under the mentorship of Prof. Joshua Hartshorne.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing video games 🎮, watching movies 🎬, hiking 🚶, golfing ⛳️, and ice skating ⛸️.
